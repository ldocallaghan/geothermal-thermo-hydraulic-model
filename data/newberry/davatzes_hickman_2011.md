# Davatzes and Hickman (2011): NWG 55-29, Newberry

Davatzes, N.C., Hickman, S.H. (2011). "Preliminary Analysis of Stress in the
Newberry EGS Well NWG 55-29." *GRC Transactions* 35. Geothermal Data
Repository, submission 737: https://gdr.openei.org/files/737/davatzes-and-hickman-2011.pdf

Read from the paper. Figure values are marked (fig.).

## Well and log

- TD 10,040 ft MD GL (3,066.3 m). Open hole from the casing shoe at 6,435 ft
  to TD (shoe shown at 6,446 ft TVD in their Fig. 4).
- Extrusive basalt, andesite, rhyolite and welded tuffs, intruded by
  granodiorite and dacite below about 8,610 ft MD GL; a sharp transition to
  massive granodiorite at about 8,807 ft MD GL.
- ALT ABI85 televiewer to 8,860 ft MD GL, after an inject-to-cool programme,
  "at a maximum temperature of 277 °C". The log spans the upper 2,425 ft of the
  3,629 ft open hole.
- Static water table at 420 ft (128 m) TVD GL.

## Breakouts

- Shmin azimuth 092.3 ± 17.3°. Statistical mode of breakout width 35.86°.
- Breakouts in the volcanics; none in the granodiorite (their Fig. 3).

## Stress model

- Hoop stress σθθ = SHmax + Shmin − 2(SHmax − Shmin)cos 2θ − 2Pf − ΔP + σthermal,
  with ΔP = Pm − Pf. Breakouts form where σθθ ≥ UCS: they argue the pore
  pressure next to the wall equals the borehole fluid pressure, so UCS is the
  right criterion.
- No direct Shmin measurement. Injection tests during the inject-to-cool
  operation (wellhead pressures up to 1,153 psi) caused no hydrofracture, which
  gives a lower bound on Shmin. Shmin is set at the critical value for normal
  faulting, Shmin = (Sv − Pf)/[(μ² + 1)^½ + μ]² + Pf, for μ from 0.38 to 0.85,
  with 0.55 and 0.70 used for the SHmax models.
- SHmax from breakout width and porosity-derived UCS, assuming minimum
  borehole fluid pressure (Pm = Pf) and **zero thermal stress**, which "would
  contribute a tension to σθθ that would also inhibit breakout formation".
- Linear SHmax fits (fig., psi with z the TVD in ft): 0.772·z + 1112.6 for
  μ = 0.55; 0.76336·z + 1094.1 for μ = 0.70.
- Stress state at 8,420 ft MD GL (their Fig. 6, μ = 0.55, σthermal = 0):
  Sv 8,852.9 psi, Pf 3,121.6 psi, Pm 3,121.6 psi, Shmin 4,675.3 psi,
  SHmax 7,305.1 psi. Pf/Sv ≈ 0.34 in the open hole (their Table 2).
- Normal faulting, with SHmax below Sv over the open hole.

## Rock strength

- UCS = 13,800·exp(−0.04744·φ) psi, φ the porosity in percent, fitted to
  compiled UCS–porosity data and anchored to a welded-tuff failure envelope
  from well GEO-N2 at 4,281 ft (A. Ghassemi, pers. comm.). Applied to the
  neutron porosity log.
- UCS from 6,000 to 12,000 psi in the open hole, averaging about 10,000 psi at
  the modelled depth (text); "most likely" 8,000 to 12,000 psi (their Table 2).

## Not in the paper

- The wall temperature during logging, other than the 277 °C maximum, and the
  static formation temperature at those depths.
- Porosity by rock type, other than as a log trace (their Fig. 4).
- A mud weight: their model uses the equilibrated fluid profile (Pm = Pf).
